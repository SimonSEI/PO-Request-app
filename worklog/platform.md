## What it is

The shared shell all apps hang off: one Flask app (`app.py`, ~39k lines) on Railway, gunicorn 2 workers x 4 threads, SQLite at `$DATA_DIR/po_requests.db` on a persistent volume. Roles: `admin`, `office`, `technician` (service or install), `property_manager`. Dashboard of app cards plus a "Features Coming Soon" folder. CSRF protection, login rate limiting, hashed passwords, Spanish toggle.

## Where it stands (as of 2026-10-13)

- Passwords no longer shown on admin/technician pages (2026-10-01).

## Open threads / next steps
