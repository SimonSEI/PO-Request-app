## What it is

Two parts: (1) a forecast of when snowbirds return to Naples (temperature gap between Naples and northern origin cities vs Collier traffic), and (2) a clients tool that finds clients with a second home up north and plans discounted quote resends timed to their return. The clients tool never writes to Jobber; client data lives on its own Railway volume, not in GitHub.

## Where it stands (as of 2026-10-13)

- Forecast dashboard and 2026 season report shipped 2026-09-15; county picker added.
- Clients tool: send queue with "Send now", resend discount (never on monthly maintenance quotes), do-not-send list, auto-send kill switch.
