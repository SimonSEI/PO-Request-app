## What it is

Monthly irrigation maintenance entry per community. Techs fill line items by zone/clock (with Spanish UI and bad-WiFi-safe saving); the office reviews submissions, sets per-community or default part pricing, and exports billing to Excel (aggregated by zone, "All good" vs "Not serviced" status). Communities can have clocks with address lists imported from Excel, custom tabs, and can now be archived.

## Where it stands (as of 2026-10-13)

- Archive communities with an Archived Communities tab (latest change, Oct 2026).
- Deleted submissions kept 60 days with restore; Active Drafts tab with read-only review.

## Open threads / next steps
