## What it is

Techs request purchase orders against a job (PO numbers like `I-1 Herons` for install, `S-...` for service, with store name and client). Vendor invoices arriving in PO@ (or uploaded) are read (text, OCR, Davis/SiteOne layouts), and matched to POs by number, fuzzy text, then Claude with a confidence score. Hourly mailbox check. Office sees matched POs, can undo matches, manage jobs/techs and budgets (Cost of Materials).
