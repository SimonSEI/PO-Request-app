## What it is

Chases open invoices from the QuickBooks A/R sheet (Aging Detail or the Aging Summary with the office's typed notes). Groups invoices into install jobs vs service calls, tracks retainage, sends staged follow-ups (friendly -> past due -> very past due -> final), reads replies, and watches lien deadlines. Hourly cycle (`AR_AUTO_RUN`).

## Where it stands (as of 2026-10-13)

- Retainage only on install invoices, any open share near 5-10% (2-12%).
- Notice of Nonpayment only at 40+ days late and always approved by a person.
- Reads sent mail too and says who each invoice is waiting on.
