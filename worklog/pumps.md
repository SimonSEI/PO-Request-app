## What it is

Tracks every pump, diver/lake, filter and SCADA job from request to invoice. Pump work is subcontracted to **Wettech** (Tommy), lakes/divers to **Gulfshore** (Jordan). Flow: request -> vendor quote -> our Jobber quote (+30%, one "Service Proposal Amount" line) -> client approves -> vendor works -> vendor bill -> our draft invoice at the quoted price -> client pays -> pay vendor. Sarasota County is billed the government way (+10%, PUMP SERVICE line with WO/PO, no tax). Tabs: Today, Home, SCADA, Accounts, Reports. Schedulers: mailbox scan every 30 min, Jobber sync, hourly jobs, 8am weekday digest email.

## Where it stands (as of 2026-10-13)

- Accounts each have a full page (setup, Jobber client/property/maintenance job links, to-dos, jobs, documents).
- "Maintenance this month" shows one row per account with Wettech and Gulfshore notified/not side by side; several visits can be ticked and marked notified at once.
- Jobber sync also picks up approved/sent quotes whose title doesn't say pump when lines mention pump/Wettech or the account is followed.
- Job status updates (parts ordered, scheduled, work done...), vendor follow-up tracking, quote signing (Word quote stamped "Simon Weardon <date>"), Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo.
- OpenClaw API exists but is **off** (`PUMPS_OPENCLAW=true` re-enables).

## Open threads / next steps

- Some Jobber mutations (report attachments, `quoteCreate` tax settings, `searchTerm`) are not yet confirmed against the live account - see PUMPS_README "Not yet confirmed".
- Hard rule: the app never sends anything to a client; quotes and invoices are drafts only.
