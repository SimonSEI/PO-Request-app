11 · What it's doing right now

The quoting loop, step by step

Step 1DiscoveryPulls every quote sitting in draft status in Jobber
Step 2TriageReads the internal notes. Materials and scope from a technician? Process it. Nothing there? Skip it — untouched
Step 3Title"ESTIMATE - IRRIGATION" becomes "Bermad Solenoid Installation"
Step 4Build the linesMaterials with quantities, crew labor converted to the right unit, and a scope paragraph — batched to Jobber's catalog matcher, which links each item to Products & Services and pulls pricing
Step 5Clean upDrops the placeholder "PROPOSAL AMOUNT SERVICE" line and leaves an internal note recording exactly what it changed
Step 6Hand offPosts a summary in #quotes-review so Beatriz knows which quotes are ready to price
12 · Guardrails

Where it deliberately stops

The agent does the assembly. It does not do the judgement, and it does not touch the client.

✕Doesn't set final prices — that stays with Beatriz and the office
✕Doesn't send anything to a client
✕Won't touch a quote that has no technician notes
✕Never invents materials — every line comes off the tech's list

The work it hands over is a quote that's been read, named, itemised and documented. A person still decides what it's worth.
